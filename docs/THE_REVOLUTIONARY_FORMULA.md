# The revolutionary formula

Three things, not one, are what set this repo's approach apart from other AI harnesses.

- **Humans collaborating, not just producing.** Most "work better with AI" advice — coding assistants, integrations, personal productivity tools — makes one person faster at their own work; the point here is a *group* of people working a problem together, with chat and this repo as what connects them ([`groups-not-individuals`](OUR_PHILOSOPHY.md#groups-not-individuals)).
- **Protocols Generated Automatically (Then Reviewed By Humans).** The rules that govern how the work gets done are extracted automatically, as a side effect of doing the work, rather than written up separately afterward ([`protocols-generated-not-just-documented`](REASONS_WHY.md#protocols-generated-not-just-documented)).
- **Chat as the only way in.** A person's way of touching any document or artifact here is to talk to Claude and have Claude make the change, never to open the file and edit it directly ([`ai-chat-as-intermediary`](COMPANY_BUILDING_RULES.md#ai-chat-as-intermediary)).

None of the three is new on its own; together, they make collaborative work look and feel different from anything a solo-developer-focused harness sets out to do.
